# Kaizrug OS Products

Kaizrug OS produces templates, services, audits, guides, and business systems.

## Product ladder

### Tier 1 — Free

Public proof and education.

Includes:

- Framework maps
- Architecture diagrams
- Public guides
- GitHub documentation
- Public screenshots

### Tier 2 — Low ticket

Self-serve digital products.

Includes:

- Notion templates
- Open Loop systems
- Personal OS starter kits
- Command Center starter kit
- Hospitality Pro Hub

### Tier 3 — Professional

Done-with-you or done-for-you systems.

Includes:

- Documentation audits
- Notion system builds
- Operations mapping
- Business documentation systems
- Hospitality knowledge systems

### Tier 4 — Strategic

Business and ecosystem systems.

Includes:

- Royal Connect Africa
- Export systems
- Tourism systems
- Community infrastructure
- Business operating systems
- Partner/vendor knowledge systems

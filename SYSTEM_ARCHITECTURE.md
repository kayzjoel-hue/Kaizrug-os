# KAIZRUG OS Architecture

## Mission
KAIZRUG OS is a personal operating system for managing projects, workflows, business operations, learning, branding, and automation.

## Core Modules

### KAIZRUG HQ
Central command center for planning, dashboards, and operations.

### Pipeline Pro
Lead tracking, opportunity management, workflow pipelines, and execution tracking.

### Booking System
Business booking and reservation workflows.

### CV & Portfolio Systems
Professional branding, resumes, and portfolio presentation.

### Royal Connect Africa
Community, branding, networking, and business initiatives.

## Future Expansion
- AI agents
- Knowledge management
- Hospitality operations tools
- CRM systems
- Business intelligence dashboards
- Export and trade workflows

## Guiding Principle
Build once. Document clearly. Improve continuously. Reuse systems across projects.
